# "Because You Know My Name"
*A Song in the Style of Jim Reeves*
*Reimagined from "Because I Love You"*

**Style:** Smooth Country Ballad  
**Tempo:** ♩ = 72 BPM  
**Key:** G Major  
**Notes:** Lush string arrangements, gentle acoustic guitar, classic Nashville sound

---

[Verse 1]
Because you see the shadows
I carry in my soul
You find me where I am
And somehow make me whole

[Verse 2]
Because you built a shelter
From winter's bitter cold
Where I can simply be
Loved just as I came

[Bridge]
Though voices rise around me
Though doubt surrounds like night
Still you keep believing
Still you hold the light

[Verse 3]
Because through wind and weather
Your love has stayed the same
I'm held within your keeping
Because you know my name

[Tag]
Held within your keeping
Because you know my name
