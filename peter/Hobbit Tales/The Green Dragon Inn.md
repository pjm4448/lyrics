By the old stone bridge in Bywater fair,
Where the Brandywine flows soft and slow,
Stands the Green Dragon with lantern bright,
And a welcome sign swinging to and fro.

The hearth burns high with a crackling flame,
Tankards brimmed with nut-brown ale,
Mushroom pies and a wheel of cheese,
While the pipe-weed smoke curls like a tale.

[Chorus]
Oh the Green Dragon, the Green Dragon!
Where hobbits gather and merry voices ring!
With ale and song till the night grows long,
At the Green Dragon we laugh and sing!

The farmers tramp from the Southfarthing fields,
Millers shake off their dusty boots,
We share the news of the latest crop
And jest at the ways of the Big Folk’s roots.

Fiddles strike up a lively air,
Boots stamp time on the old oak floor,
We dance a jig till our cheeks glow red,
And call for another round or more.

[Chorus]
Oh the Green Dragon, the Green Dragon!
Where hobbits gather and merry voices ring!
With ale and song till the night grows long,
At the Green Dragon we laugh and sing!

When the stars shine clear and the night grows deep,
We lift our voices one last cheer,
To the Shire’s peace and her good green earth,
At the Green Dragon we hold most dear.
