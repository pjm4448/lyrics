# "The Price of Our Release"
### The Watchtower - January 2026

---

[INTRO]
*(Fingerpicked acoustic guitar, building softly)*

---

[VERSE 1]
When Adam turned away from Jehovah's hand,
He passed a sinful nature to every child of man.
We're trapped beneath the rubble, we can't lift the weight,
Inherited and helpless, we could only cry and wait.

---

[PRE-CHORUS]
But Jehovah heard our crying,
And He answered with a plan,
He gave His only Son to pay the ransom price for sinful man.

---

[CHORUS]
The ransom is the reason I can stand before Him clean,
The blood of Jesus Christ our Lord, the greatest gift I've known.
It gave Jehovah legal ground to wash my sins away,
And bring me back to His embrace and keep me there to stay.
The price of our release was paid by Jesus on that day.

---

[VERSE 2]
Our sinful state runs deeper than the wrong that we have done,
It makes us weak and weary underneath the burning sun.
Disease and grief and aging all trace back to Adam's fall,
But Jehovah's new world promise says He'll heal us, one and all.

---

[PRE-CHORUS]
So I'm holding to the anchor,
Sure and firm through every gale,
This hope that Jehovah granted will not falter, will not fail.

[CHORUS]

He's rich in mercy, rich in love,
He answered Satan's challenge from above,
His name is sanctified, His purpose clear,
The God of hope has drawn His children near.

[Production Notes]

Tempo: 72-78 BPM, slow country gospel ballad feel. Key of G or A major. Male lead vocal; delivery should be earnest and grateful throughout. Acoustic guitar carries the verses with gentle fingerpicked patterns. Electric guitar adds subtle pedal steel or slide fills on the pre-choruses. A full band with light snare, brushed drums, and bass enters on the first chorus. Piano supports the bridge, building in emotional intensity before pulling back. The final chorus features all instruments at full volume, creating an anthemic swell, before a gentle acoustic guitar fade on the outro. Background vocals on choruses add warmth and depth. A light tambourine or shaker keeps rhythmic feel through the verses. Estimated runtime: 4:30 to 5:00. Structure: Intro, Verse 1, Pre-Chorus, Chorus, Verse 2, Pre-Chorus, Chorus, Bridge, Final Chorus, Outro.
