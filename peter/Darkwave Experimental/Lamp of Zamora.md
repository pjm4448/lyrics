[Verse 1]
In Zamora, under stone,
I sell my prayers for bread.
Coins like ash in my palm,
and mercy long since fled.

The silk men come at dusk,
with rings and wine-stained hands.
They name their hungers soft,
then take what I can stand.

[Pre-Chorus]
I keep my face like iron,
my heart behind the door.
One more night, one more mask,
one more step across the floor.

[Chorus]
Zamora, swallow me slow
Zamora, I still go
(Oh, Zamora)
Count the coins, count the sins
I live where the black river bends

[Verse 2]
Some want tears in silence,
some want a knife-shaped smile.
Some want me to kneel low
and call them lord awhile.

I know their hidden shame,
I know their altar trade.
I wear my hunger like chains,
and still I do not break.

[Bridge]
If the moon should ask my name,
I’ll spit it in the dust.
I was carved by need and loss,
but I am more than lust.

There’s a girl beneath the bruise,
there’s fire under bone.
If the gates still stand at dawn,
I will walk out alone.
