**Track 7 – Chapter 6: Lucien and the Gilded Rot of Ages**

**Verse 1**  
The House of Ormuz stands as a gilded mausoleum  
Dust thick as cerements chokes every room  
Tapestries sag heavy with mildew and faded pride  
Lucien wastes away where his father once thrived  

**Pre-Chorus**  
Sea green silks now frayed and stained with filth  
Slave girls have fled his decaying wealth  
He clutches a vial in his trembling skeletal hand  
The golden powder whispers its cursed demand  

**Chorus**  
Gilded rot, gilded rot of ages  
Once proud merchant heir now trapped in its cages  
Deeper he falls into the dust’s cold embrace  
Aurea’s poison slowly steals his final grace  

**Verse 2**  
Jafar returns with silken words that deceive  
Another carved box of golden dust for the lost who grieve  
The dream grows darker with each forbidden breath  
The feasting hall twists into visions of death  

**Bridge**  
Columns of sunlight pulse with sickening light  
Companions smile with teeth too sharp and bright  
Golden smears burn his flesh as the vision breaks  
He wakes with fresh scars that the waking world takes  

**Chorus**  
Gilded rot, gilded rot of ages  
Once proud merchant heir now trapped in its cages  
Deeper he falls into the dust’s cold embrace  
Aurea’s poison slowly steals his final grace  

**Outro**  
Weeks bleed into ruin in a filth-choked tavern low  
Lucien hunches broken over cheap imitation glow  
His coffers lie empty and his spirit long dead  
The golden decay claims another soul instead  

**Production Notes**  
Decadent darkwave evolving into industrial decay at a slow 60-70 BPM. Start with the same shimmering golden arpeggiated synths from Track 2, but now heavily warped, detuned, and buried under grinding industrial loops and distant coughing textures. Breathy female backing vocals from the earlier track return but sound ragged and mocking. Use cold piano strikes for the shattering visions and add low, rumbling sub-bass drones to represent the growing emptiness. In the bridge, introduce swirling, dissonant synths and a burning high-frequency shimmer effect for the golden smears. The outro shifts to a sparse, hollow sound with faint tavern noise in the background and a final, fading golden sparkle that turns sour and distorted. Vocals begin intimate and listless, growing more ragged and desperate by the outro to mirror Lucien’s physical and spiritual collapse. The production should feel opulent at first, then increasingly rotten and suffocating.
