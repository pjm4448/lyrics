# This Small and Sacred Time

**Verse 1:**
Today, while the coffee slowly steams
And morning light spills through the blinds
I'll set aside my restless dreams
And let this moment fill my mind

**Chorus:**
Today, I will live within this hour
Not yesterday's regret or tomorrow's chase
Today, I'll feel the gentle power
Of simply being in this place

**Verse 2:**
Today, I'll walk the long way home
And notice what I've hurried past
The way the autumn maples glow
As if each leaf could be the last

**Chorus:**

**Verse 3:**
Today, I'll reach out to a friend
And say the words I've meant to say
For nothing ever stays the same
And all we're promised is today

**Bridge:**
The world will spin its worried way
The news will shout, the clocks will chime
But here beside you I will stay
And hold this small and sacred time

**Final Verse:**
Today, when evening shadows fall
And stars appear like scattered seed
I'll know I had enough of all
That any heart could ever need
