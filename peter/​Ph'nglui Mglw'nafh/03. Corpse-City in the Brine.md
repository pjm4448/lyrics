​[Verse 1]
[Slow, massive doom riff. Low, mournful clean vocals. 4/4 time]
A seismic shudder tears the ocean floor awake
An island thrust from torment as the planetary pillars break
Johansen steers the Emma through a foul and oily foam
To tread a nightmare acropolis where mortals cannot roam.
We touch the oozy masonry of an epoch dead and gone
A slime-slick, cyclopean tomb that chokes the rising dawn.

​[Pre-Chorus]
[Tempo slowing further. Crushing bass weight. Dissonant guitars.]
The angles warp and stagger, the stone is false and wrong
A non-Euclidean labyrinth that sings a silent song.
Acute turns into obtuse... the geometry deceives...
The horror swallows every soul that breathes.

​[Chorus]
[Monumental doom tempo. Deep, abyssal growls mixed with operatic swells.]
R'lyeh rises from the black, a corpse-city in the brine!
The vault is wide and broken, we have crossed the cosmic line!
Out from the cyclopean hatch, the mountain walks the wave
A squamous, green monstrosity to make the earth his grave!
The titan stalks the shattered sea, a monument of dread.

​[Verse 2]
[Instruments drop to a lone, heavy bass line. Frantic, desperate vocals.]
The crewmen fall like insects in the shadow of his stride
Sucked down into the shifting stone or swallowed by the tide.
Only Johansen grips the wheel, the steam-pipes screaming high
He rams the charging iron yacht against the mountain in the sky!
A desperate strike of tearing steel through wet, necrotic space
Slicing through the putrid jelly of a god without a face!

​[Bridge]
[Maximum distortion. Ultra-slow funeral doom breakdown.]
But the cloven mist recombines...
The cosmic structure re-aligns...
The vaporous wounds heal fast as thought.
Our final stand was all for naught.

​[Chorus]
[Full orchestral and metal explosion. Layered growls and operatic wails.]
R'lyeh rises from the black, a corpse-city in the brine!
The vault is wide and broken, we have crossed the cosmic line!
Out from the cyclopean hatch, the mountain walks the wave
A squamous, green monstrosity to make the earth his grave!
The titan stalks the shattered sea, a monument of dread.

​[Outro]
[Guitars fade to feedback. Slow, heavy oceanic waves crash repeatedly.]
The black tides pull him down again.
The water seals the tomb.
The ocean hides the ancient shame.
The world awaits its doom.
