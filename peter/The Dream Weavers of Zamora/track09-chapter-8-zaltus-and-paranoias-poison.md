**Track 9 – Chapter 8: Zaltus and Paranoia’s Poison**

**Verse 1**  
In the heavy air of his stone-walled den  
Zaltus the captain paces like a wolf in a pen  
His once iron web of spies and brutal might  
Now unravels in failure beneath starless night  

**Pre-Chorus**  
Ambushers ambushed, informants slain with throats slit wide  
Ophir gold vanished, secrets stolen from his side  
He bellows at his men, fists crashing on the oak  
Suspicion poisons every order he spoke  

**Chorus**  
Paranoia’s poison seeps into his veins  
Twisting his mind till no loyalty remains  
Every shadow hides a traitor’s blade  
The Black Hand fractures beneath the weight of his rage  

**Verse 2**  
He turns on Gorgos with eyes burning red  
Accusing the loyal of betrayal instead  
Then draws his sword on Borin in a berserk haze  
Blood sprays the floor in his paranoid craze  

**Bridge**  
The Hand that once ruled through fear and gold  
Now tears itself apart from within its hold  
Zaltus sees treason in every face and breath  
His own madness forging the instrument of death  

**Chorus**  
Paranoia’s poison seeps into his veins  
Twisting his mind till no loyalty remains  
Every shadow hides a traitor’s blade  
The Black Hand fractures beneath the weight of his rage  

**Outro**  
Unseen above the city the carrion bird wheels high  
Sensing the feast of chaos brewing in the sky  
Zaltus’s empire crumbles from his own trembling hand  
Paranoia’s poison devours the captain where he stands  

**Production Notes**  
Dark industrial with paranoid techno elements at a mid-tempo 85-95 BPM. Mechanical heartbeat kick drum drives the track, layered with whispering voices panning aggressively left and right to create unease. Sudden stabs of distorted radio static and grinding metallic textures represent the growing suspicion. Vocals start as a low, menacing growl in the verses and build to raw, unhinged shouting in the chorus. In the bridge, add rising feedback and chaotic layered screams to mirror the eruption of violence. The outro fades into distant, circling bird-like cries mixed with low sub-bass drones. Overall production should feel claustrophobic and unstable, with constant tension that never fully resolves, pulling the listener into Zaltus’s fracturing sanity and the self-destructive collapse of the Black Hand.
