[Verse 1]
Smoke on the broken hill
Iron and ash on the air
I found you under the ruin
Blood in your braided hair
Your hand reached out through the armor
Shaking, still holding a crown
You smiled that stubborn smile
And finally laid it down

[Chorus]
Lay him in stone and snow
Under the mountain’s cold shadow
All of his silver, all of his gold
Cannot buy back the word he spoke low
“Friend, will you stay
Till I must go?”
Lay him in stone and snow

[Verse 2]
You said you’d measured me small
Called me a thief in your hall
Now in the dust between heartbeats
You asked forgiveness for all
I mumbled jokes through the sobbing
(you always hated my tears)
You laughed and gripped me harder
Then faded out of my years

[Chorus]
Lay him in stone and snow
Under the mountain’s cold shadow
All of this shining, terrible hoard
Cannot mend what this sorrow has torn
Still I will stay
Till you must go
Lay him in stone and snow

[Bridge]
They gave you the heart of the mountain
Cold as the stars above
Set it against your quiet chest
As if it could answer love
I turned my pockets inside out
Crumbs and a broken pipe
Nothing to pay this bitter price
Nothing to make it right

[Chorus]
Lay him in stone and snow
Under the mountain’s cold shadow
All of the jewels the deep can afford
Cannot sweeten this hard, hurting road
So I turn away
Back to my home
