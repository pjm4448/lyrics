# "Some Nights Are Silver"

**Verse 1:**
I have walked through fields of clover
I have stumbled over ground
I have felt the warmth of summer
And the cold when it comes around
There were times I thought I had it
Had this life all figured out
Then the morning brought me questions
Filled my certainty with doubt

**Chorus:**
Some nights are silver, some nights are grey
Some roads are easy, some lead astray
You take the bitter right along with the sweet
Some nights you're dancing, some nights you weep

**Verse 2:**
I have loved and felt the wonder
Of a heart that beats with mine
I have lost and felt the empty
Spaces left along the line
There were moments bright as diamonds
There were hours dark as coal
But I learned that both are needed
To make a person whole

**Bridge:**
And I'm not asking for the answers
I've stopped looking for the key
I just wake up every morning
And let the day be what it will be

**Verse 3:**
Now the face inside my mirror
Shows the years that I have known
Every wrinkle tells a story
Of the seeds that I have sown
Some fell on rock and withered
Some took root and learned to grow
That's the harvest life has offered
The only truth I know

**Final Chorus:**
Some nights are silver, some nights are grey
Some roads are easy, some lead astray
You take the bitter right along with the sweet
Some nights you're dancing, some nights you weep
Some nights are silver, some nights are stone
But every night leads me closer to home
