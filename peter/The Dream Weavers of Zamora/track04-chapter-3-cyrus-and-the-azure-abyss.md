**Track 4 – Chapter 3: Cyrus and the Azure Abyss**

**Verse 1**  
In the crumbling noble quarter where ghosts cling to stone  
Cyrus the last scion claims the bloodline as his own  
Threadbare cloak and rusty blade held in trembling hand  
Delusional fire burns where no real power stands  

**Pre-Chorus**  
He rails at faded frescoes in the dust-choked hall  
Proclaiming empires and glory that will never fall  
By the black blood of Vanth his ancestors carved stone  
Yet he stands alone, a madman on a broken throne  

**Chorus**  
Azure abyss, azure abyss  
Power calls from the cold and endless mist  
He craves the vision to seize his rightful throne  
A minor noble lost inside a dream unknown  

**Verse 2**  
Farid the Seeker enters with eyes of glacial blue  
Voice like ice that slices every promise in two  
He offers the Azure Draught from a leaden casket cold  
A tool for the ambitious whose hunger grows bold  

**Bridge**  
Cyrus inhales and the world fractures wide  
He stands on a black parapet with banners snapping in pride  
An army millions strong beneath an azure sky  
Crown of glowing crystals as ancestors bow high  

**Chorus**  
Azure abyss, azure abyss  
Power calls from the cold and endless mist  
He craves the vision to seize his rightful throne  
A minor noble lost inside a dream unknown  

**Outro**  
He wakes on the cold slab with azure stains on his palms  
The god-like roar still echoes like distant alarms  
His squalid life now feels like a cruel jest  
The memory of dominion burns within his chest  

**Production Notes**  
Dungeon synth with orchestral power drone elements at a slow 60-70 BPM. Cold, regal polysynths and distant war-horn calls create an ancient and majestic atmosphere. Azure vision section features massive layered choir and thunderous timpani that build to a peak before cutting abruptly into a thin, reedy voice for Cyrus's delusions. Use low-pass filtered strings and subtle glitch effects in the outro to represent the fall into ash and disillusionment. Keep vocals spoken-sung with theatrical grandeur in the verses, shifting to desperate hunger in the chorus. The overall sound should feel grandiose yet hollow, drawing the listener from pathetic ambition into intoxicating fantasy and back to bitter reality. No heavy percussion until the bridge, then let it drop sharply afterward to emphasize the contrast between dream and waking squalor.
