**Track 6 – Chapter 5: Tariq and the Red Feast**

**Verse 1**  
In the crime-ridden underbelly where honor dies fast  
Tariq wanders broken by betrayal from the past  
Scars cross his arms from forgotten border fights  
But the deepest wounds burn from his comrades’ treacherous bites  

**Pre-Chorus**  
Left to bleed in the Stygian mud by Captain Borus  
Laughter of jackals as they stripped the dying from us  
Now in Zamora he hunts with vengeance in his veins  
A gaunt grim wraith driven by unrelenting pains  

**Chorus**  
Red feast, red feast  
Heart of the Berserker grants the savage release  
Blood for blood and bones ground into dust  
The powder turns hatred to a weapon forged in rust  

**Verse 2**  
Kaelen the Blood-Smith waits in his iron forge deep  
Mountain of muscle with a cloak hiding secrets he keeps  
He offers the coarse red powder that burns like flame  
Forged from the rage of a warrior god and blood sacrifice’s shame  

**Bridge**  
Tariq inhales and the desert canyon ignites  
Borus and his traitors appear in the flickering light  
He charges like a demon with an inhuman roar  
Ripping skulls and tearing limbs on the blood-soaked floor  

**Chorus**  
Red feast, red feast  
Heart of the Berserker grants the savage release  
Blood for blood and bones ground into dust  
The powder turns hatred to a weapon forged in rust  

**Outro**  
He wakes in the den with hands slick and warm with fresh red  
The dream felt so real yet the blood stains his hands instead  
A carrion bird circles with ruby eyes so cold  
Marking the warrior whose soul the red feast has sold  

**Production Notes**  
Doom metal with berserker black metal elements at a mid tempo 85-95 BPM that accelerates into blast beats during the bridge. Crushing down tuned guitars and tribal war drums build the tension, exploding into chaotic intensity when the powder hits. Layer guttural roars and throat singing for Tariq’s rage. Use wet gore sound effects and heavy distortion on vocals during the dream sequence. The carrion bird motif appears as distant, distorted guitar feedback. Overall production should feel raw, brutal, and visceral, shifting from simmering hatred to explosive bloodlust and ending on a note of chilling realization. Vocals are harsh and growled in the verses, becoming unhinged and bestial in the bridge and chorus.
