# Happy Are Those Who Seek
### The Watchtower - January 2026

[Verse 1]
There’s a hunger deep inside my soul,
A longing only God’s truth can make me whole.
I search for wisdom, seek Jehovah’s way,
His loving guidance brightens every day.

[Chorus]
Happy am I, for I seek my spiritual need,
With God’s Word as the daily bread I heed.
Drawing close to Him in prayer and deed,
I find true peace, my heart is freed.

[Verse 2]
The world may offer pleasures that won’t last,
But treasures from Jehovah hold me fast.
With brothers, sisters, gathered in His name,
We build each other up, our faith aflame.

[Chorus]
Happy am I, for I seek my spiritual need,
With God’s Word as the daily bread I heed.
Drawing close to Him in prayer and deed,
I find true peace, my heart is freed.

[Bridge]
When trials come and shadows cloud my view,
I turn to God, His promises are true.
He fills my life with purpose, hope, and light,
And helps me walk by faith and not by sight.

[Chorus]
Happy am I, for I seek my spiritual need,
With God’s Word as the daily bread I heed.
Drawing close to Him in prayer and deed,
I find true peace, my heart is freed.

[Outro]
Each day I strive to draw close in prayer,
My spiritual need - Jehovah’s love and care.

---

Production Notes / Musical Direction:
- Tempo: Moderate, uplifting country ballad (80–90 BPM)
- Instrumentation: Acoustic guitar, gentle percussion, pedal steel, upright bass, light piano
- Vocal style: Warm, sincere, and earnest; harmonies on chorus and outro
- Arrangement: Verses with simple guitar, chorus with fuller instrumentation, bridge with dynamic build, soft outro
- Mood: Encouraging, peaceful, and hopeful, reflecting the joy and fulfillment of satisfying one’s spiritual need
