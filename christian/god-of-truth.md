# "God of Truth"
### A Christian Pop Rock Song

---

## INTRO
*(Atmospheric guitars, building drums)*

---

## VERSE 1
In a world where lies surround us,
Where the serpent's schemes abound,
There's a Maker of the heavens,
On His promises we stand.

He created us in His image,
Gave us life and gave us home,
Every word He speaks is certain—
Not one promise overthrown.

---

## PRE-CHORUS
He's the Source, He's the Sustainer,
His record stands through every age—

---

## CHORUS
He's the God of truth, He always fulfills His purpose,
Nothing formed against His name will ever stand.
From the ransom of His Son to paradise restored,
Every plan is written by His hand.
He's the God of truth—fulfiller of every promise,
And His love will never fail.

---

## VERSE 2
Though the enemy rages fiercely,
Knowing time is running short,
Angels guide the ones who're seeking,
Gathering souls from every nation.

From the nations comes a great crowd,
Lovers of the truth unite,
Babylon will fall to silence,
When the King completes His fight.

---

## CHORUS

## BRIDGE
*(Stripped back, then building intensity)*

Not death, nor life, nor any creation,
Not rulers, nor powers can separate—
His love holds fast through tribulation,
In His hands, our future's safe.

Vindication's coming for His name,
Every liar swept away,
We will worship Him alone—
Truth will reign forever!

---

## FINAL CHORUS
*(Full band, anthemic, all instruments)*

He's the God of truth, He always fulfills His purpose,
Nothing formed against His name will ever stand!
From the ransom of His Son to paradise restored,
Every plan is written by His hand.
He's the God of truth—fulfiller of every promise,
And His love will never fail!

*(Repeat with ad-libs)*
His love will never fail…
His love will never fail!

---

## OUTRO / TAG
*(Fading, reflective)*

God of truth… God of truth…
Always fulfills His purpose.
God of truth… God of truth…
And His love will never fail.
