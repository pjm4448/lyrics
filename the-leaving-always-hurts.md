# "The Leaving Always Hurts"

**Verse 1:**
The suitcase sits beside the door
Your silence fills the room
I watch you standing by the window
In the pale light of the moon
We have spoken all the reasons
We have held each other tight
But knowing why I have to go
Does not make it feel right
