[Verse 1]
In the Northfarthing spring, under cloud-tattered sky
Where the barley ran gold, where the larks nested high
Came a shadow of war to the turf and the tilled
And the grass drank a fear it had never yet filled

They were farmers and millers with pitchfork and pan
Who had never yet marched like the tall folk of Man
But they whispered one name as the foe crossed the hills
“Bandobras Took, come and settle our ills”

[Chorus]
Raise your cups for the Bullroarer Took
Big as a story, wide as a brook
He laughed like thunder, he charged like rain
And he turned the Green Fields green again
Oh Bullroarer, bold and free
Stomped that line through the barley sea
From hobbit-door to distant brook
Sing Bandobras, Bullroarer Took

[Verse 2]
He bestrode that wild steed like a lord out of tales
Curls in the wind, cloak snapping like sails
With a club in his hand as gnarled as an oak
And a grin that bit through the gathering smoke

“Back to your holes, you crow-helmed horde
You have nettled the Shire and you’ll answer my word”
Then the hillfolk ran shouting to his ragged command
Just a ring of small feet on a trembling land
