[Verse 1]
Stone dust on his shoulders
Ash in his beard
Echoes in the blackness
Dripping from the spears
He turns once on the stairway
Fire at his heels
We’re twelve hearts and one heartbeat
Too afraid to heal

[Chorus]
Fly, you fools
His voice cuts the dark in two
As the bridge gives way beneath him
And he falls from view
Fly, you fools
But our feet are made of stone
We are running through the caverns
And we’re suddenly alone

[Verse 2]
A shadow crowned in embers
A terror older than our tongues
His staff flares like a sunrise
In a world that has no sun
He stands there like a doorway
Between the blaze and all we love
Then the cracking of the granite
Steals the air from every lung

[Chorus]
Fly, you fools
His voice cuts the dark in two
As the bridge gives way beneath him
And he falls from view
Fly, you fools
But our hearts refuse to move
We are stumbling through the tunnels
With a grief we cannot prove

[Bridge]
We do not know the ending
Only that the road goes on
His hat, his pipe, his laughter
Pulled forever from our songs
Is this courage, is this mercy
To be cast into the flame?
We are children in a cavern
Calling out his name (Gandalf!)

[Chorus]
Fly, you fools
Still it rings inside our chests
Like a bell that keeps on breaking
But will never let us rest
Fly, you fools
He has given all he could
So we carry what is left of him
Through shadow, stone, and wood
