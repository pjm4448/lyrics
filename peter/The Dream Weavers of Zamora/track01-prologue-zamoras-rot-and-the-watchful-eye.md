**Track 1 – Prologue: Zamora’s Rot and the Watchful Eye**

**Verse 1**  
In festering alleys where the black serpents breed  
Zamora the city of thieves devours every scream  
Open sewers choke the air with filth and bile  
A pestilent shroud that smothers mile on mile  

**Pre-Chorus**  
Cheap incense burns yet never masks the reek  
Of vice and despair where the gutters leak  
Screams slice sharp through the drunken wicked laughter  
A new whisper crawls from the dream-peddlers’ quarter  

**Chorus**  
Zamora, Zamora, city of thieves and bone  
Beneath a starless sky where no dawn is ever known  
A skeletal corpse lies twisted in the door  
Golden dust still stains the lips that breathe no more  

**Verse 2**  
From tanneries by the poisoned river’s flow  
To spice stalls peddling powders that scour the soul  
The miasma thickens with the promise of no tomorrow  
A subtle dust that bends all minds to sorrow  

**Bridge**  
Above the ruin on a crumbling gargoyle’s perch  
A carrion bird keeps its ancient, patient search  
Ruby eyes gleam with cold and knowing fire  
Talons thick as thumbs, wings black as funeral pyre  

**Chorus**  
Zamora, Zamora, city of thieves and bone  
Beneath a starless sky where no dawn is ever known  
A skeletal corpse lies twisted in the door  
Golden dust still stains the lips that breathe no more  

**Outro**  
With leathery flap it descends in ritual grace  
Its shadow falls upon the golden tainted face  
A merchant spits his curses at the rot  
Yet the bird claims its feast, for every soul in Zamora feeds its hunger  
Every soul in Zamora feeds its hunger  

**Production Notes**  
Male vocals. Ultra-slow tempo at 30 BPM built on deep detuned cello drones and sub-bass rumbles that evoke endless decay. Layer faint field recordings of dripping water, distant muffled screams, and wind howling through stone alleys. A single low female choir moan drifts unresolved throughout, growing slightly during the chorus. Introduce a sparse, slightly detuned solo violin for the carrion bird motif, cold and patient. At the final outro, add realistic wing-flap sound effects that fade into complete silence, leaving only the sub-bass hum for three full seconds before cutting to black. Keep all vocals low and spoken-sung with heavy reverb to feel like a cursed storyteller whispering from the shadows. No drums, only occasional distant metallic chimes like wind through ancient gargoyles. The entire track should feel suffocating and ritualistic, immersing the listener in the city’s physical and moral rot before any character appears.
