I Made It Home

[Verse 1]
Today I loved you enough to let you go.
The hardest choice a heart can make—
to stop your pain, not mine.
I fought for more time,
but you needed peace.
Goodbye, my beautiful boy.

[Verse 2]
I made it home, Mum.
Everything is bright and new.
No more hurting.
Only love here, only play.

[Verse 3]
I'm learning to use my wings now,
running on all legs that don't ache anymore.
The angels sing,
and I am whole again.
Don't be sad—
I'm exactly where I need to be.

[Verse 4]
I made it home, Mum.
And one day, when you arrive,
I'll be the first face you see.
