# "You Come to Me Like Sunrise"

**Verse 1:**
You come to me like sunrise
Over mountains touched with gold
Like the first breath of the morning
Like a story yet untold
Like the whisper of a river
Finding passage to the sea
You have quietly surrounded
Every part of me

**Chorus:**
Let me walk beside you always
Let me hold you through the years
Let me be your shelter standing
Through the laughter and the tears
Let me wake to find you near me
Let me love you endlessly
You are all the beauty calling
Come and share this life with me

**Verse 2:**
You fill me like the moonlight
Pouring silver through the pines
Like the warmth of summer evenings
Like the sweetness of the vine
Like a meadow filled with wildflowers
Swaying gently in the breeze
You have opened up my spirit
Put my restless heart at ease
