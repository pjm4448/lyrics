# Clear My View
### The Watchtower - April 2026

---

## [INTRO]
*(Atmospheric guitar, building drums)*

## [VERSE 1]
Life is filled with trouble and sorrow
Trials come like hazards on the road
Persecution, loss, uncertain tomorrows
Family turns cold beneath this heavy load

But snow on the windshield blinds the driver
A wrong view of hardship makes us fall
I need to see clearly to survive here
To steer through the storm and stand through it all

---

## [CHORUS]
So clear my view, Jehovah, clear my view
Let me see this road the way You do
You allow but never cause the pain
Through the fire, Your love will still remain
I'll endure—Your promises are true
Nothing separates me, clear my view

Satan says we only serve for blessing

You're training me to stand, to be faithful
And my steadfast heart will make You rejoice
