# Return to My Arms

**Verse 1:**
The morning light falls soft across the bed
Where shadows of your memory still remain
I reach for you in half-forgotten dreams
And wake to find myself alone again

**Chorus:**
Return to my arms
Like the river finds the sea
Return to my arms
Come back where you belong
I've wandered far through empty days
Through sun and wind and rain
Open up your heart once more
And find your way back home

**Verse 2:**
The autumn leaves are falling in the yard
The same ones that we watched together turn
And every golden color speaks your name
A quiet fire that never ceased to burn

**Chorus:**
Return to my arms
Like the river finds the sea
Return to my arms
Come back where you belong
I've wandered far through empty days
Through sun and wind and rain
Open up your heart once more
And find your way back home

**Bridge:**
I know that I'm the one who let you go
I held on tight to foolish pride
But standing here with nothing left to show
I'd trade my pride to have you by my side

**Verse 3:**
So if you're out there listening tonight
Beneath these same old stars we used to share
Just know the porch light's on, the door's unlocked
And all my love is waiting for you here

**Final Chorus:**
Return to my arms
Like the river finds the sea
Return to my arms
You're everything to me
I'll be patient, I'll be true
Through joy or grief or pain
Just open up your heart once more
And find your way back home
