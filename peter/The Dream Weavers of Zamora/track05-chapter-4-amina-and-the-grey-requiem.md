**Track 5 – Chapter 4: Amina and the Grey Requiem**

**Verse 1**  
In Zamora where grief is as cheap as spilled wine  
Amina drifts like a wraith through the necropolis line  
Emaciated and grey in her shapeless shroud  
She haunts the graves where her family lies bowed  

**Pre-Chorus**  
Husband Sorab and children Yasmin and Kavi taken  
Stolen by sickness that left her soul forsaken  
She clutches the wooden horse worn smooth by her tears  
Whispering lullabies to the silence that sears  

**Chorus**  
Grey requiem, grey requiem  
Dust of Shadows to bridge the veil between them  
A balm for the broken heart that cannot mend  
Fleeting glimpses of the dead that only deepen the end  

**Verse 2**  
Farid finds her kneeling by moss-covered stones  
His voice soft as rain on forgotten bones  
He speaks of ghost flowers and crystallized tears  
Of thinning the veil so the lost may appear near  

**Bridge**  
She inhales the grey powder with desperate need  
The world fades to mist cold and damp as grave seed  
Faces of Sorab, Yasmin and Kavi rise from the grey  
Cold to her touch and silent as they drift away  

**Chorus**  
Grey requiem, grey requiem  
Dust of Shadows to bridge the veil between them  
A balm for the broken heart that cannot mend  
Fleeting glimpses of the dead that only deepen the end  

**Outro**  
She wakes with a choked sob on the hard pallet floor  
Wooden horse still clutched as fresh tears start to pour  
No comfort comes from the powder’s cruel art  
Only a deeper void that tears her fragile heart  

**Production Notes**  
Grey cinematic ambient with funeral drone elements at an extremely slow 40-50 BPM. Minimal percussion, relying instead on distant, tolling bells, low wind-like textures, and a single mourning cello that carries the melody with long, sustained notes. Grey powder inhalation uses a cold, sterile reverb tail that slowly engulfs the mix. Vocals should be delivered in a fragile, whispered female voice with heavy reverb and subtle delay to evoke isolation and sorrow. Keep the sound sparse and hollow, with occasional faint choral moans that never resolve. The overall production should feel damp, oppressive, and emotionally suffocating, drawing the listener into profound grief and the cruel emptiness of false comfort. No bright or warm elements; everything remains cold, distant, and decaying.
