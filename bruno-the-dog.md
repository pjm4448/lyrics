Remembering Bruno

[Verse 1]
Bruno was never just a dog to his family.
He was strength when life was heavy,
comfort when the world was unkind,
and love that never wavered.
He stayed through the hardest chapters,
through pain, change, and uncertainty,
always giving more than his body could hold.

[Chorus]
Run free, beautiful boy.
You are held in every heart that knew you,
and you will never be forgotten.

[Verse 2]
Now his work here is done.
No more aching legs.
No more struggle.
Only peace, warmth, and freedom.
He has earned his angel wings
not because he left,
but because he loved so deeply while he stayed.

[Chorus]
Run free, beautiful boy.
You are held in every heart that knew you,
and you will never be forgotten.
