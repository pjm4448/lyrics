**Track 3 – Chapter 2: Soraya and the Orchid’s Thorns**

**Verse 1**  
In Zamora’s filth where exotic flowers grow  
Soraya the dancer steals every heart in the glow  
Silks shimmer with sweat on her supple skin  
Grace that makes hardened warriors forget their sin  

**Pre-Chorus**  
Yet beneath the painted smile and flawless art  
A river of black ice tears her soul apart  
Invisible scars from a secret depraved rite  
Where pleasure and pain twist deep in the night  

**Chorus**  
Orchid’s thorns, orchid’s thorns  
Beneath the mask where her true pain is born  
She craves the power to make White Mask beg and break  
Violet dust to give her the vengeance she seeks  

**Verse 2**  
In the torch-lit cellar with leering frescoed walls  
White Mask binds her tight and forces her to crawl  
Degrading whispers and acts of utter shame  
He breaks her spirit yet she still remembers his name  

**Bridge**  
Malika the crone in her alcove of purple shade  
Offers the powder that unveils the beast she has made  
Inhale the violet and see the woman you can become  
Strike down the serpent and make the captain succumb  

**Chorus**  
Orchid’s thorns, orchid’s thorns  
Beneath the mask where her true pain is born  
She craves the power to make White Mask beg and break  
Violet dust to give her the vengeance she seeks  

**Outro**  
In the dream she stands with spectral blade in hand  
Zaltus kneels pleading across the crimson land  
Her laughter rings cruel as she strikes with raging fire  
Yet a cold whisper warns this vengeance will bind her higher  
She wakes with nails dug deep into bleeding palms  
The chains of the powder already binding her arms  

**Production Notes**  
Neoclassical darkwave with ritual dark folk elements at a slow to mid-tempo 70-80 BPM. Haunting viola and cello lines carry the melody, supported by sparse ritualistic hand percussion (bone rattles and soft frame drum). Purple powder inhalation moment uses a reversed choral swell followed by a sudden low brass stab for tension. Whispered spoken-word layers in the verses evoke the secret society rites, gradually building to breathy, venomous female vocals in the chorus. Keep the overall sound elegant yet menacing, with heavy reverb on vocals to create a sense of hidden cellars and shadowed stages. The outro features dissonant string scrapes and a fading, unresolved viola note to mirror the lingering doubt and binding chains. Production should feel seductive and dangerous, pulling the listener from public grace into private torment and vengeful hunger. Vocals shift from graceful and controlled in Verse 1 to raw and seething by the outro.
