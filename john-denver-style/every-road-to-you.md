# Every Road to You (For You)

**Verse 1:**
When morning light comes through the window glass
And shadows fade like dreams that couldn't last
I find my thoughts returning once again
To every road that led me here to you

**Chorus:**
For you, I'd walk through endless miles of rain
For you, I'd learn to love the sweetest pain
The stars don't shine as bright as what I've found
Since you came along and turned my world around

**Verse 2:**
The mountain peaks have never seemed so clear
The autumn wind sings softer when you're near
I never knew that I was incomplete
Until your heart gave mine a steady beat

**Chorus:**
For you, I'd cross the rivers running cold
For you, I'd trade the silver and the gold
There's nothing in this world I wouldn't do
To spend my every sunrise here with you

**Bridge:**
Some people search a lifetime
For what we have tonight
A love that needs no reason
A flame against the night

**Final Verse:**
So take my hand and never let it go
Through summer sun and through the winter snow
Whatever years the universe may send
For you, my love, I'll give them till the end
