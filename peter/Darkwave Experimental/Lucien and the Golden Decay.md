**Verse 1**  
In the House of Ormuz, a cage of darkened wood  
Lucien the merchant heir sits where his father stood  
Sea green silks hang loose upon his thinning frame  
Wealth without hunger, a soul consumed by shame  

**Pre-Chorus**  
Slave girls lounge like soulless painted things  
His gaze drifts vacant over forgotten kings  
He shatters a jade idol with a languid hand  
Yet the broken shards bring no thrill to this empty man  

**Chorus**  
Golden decay, golden decay  
Jaded heart that nothing can sway  
He craves the dust that promises release  
Aurea’s golden shimmer, a sweet and fleeting peace  

**Verse 2**  
Jafar the Gilded Tongue slithers through the door  
Robes of old gold with patterns that twist once more  
Metallic sweetness clings to his ageless skin  
Long golden nails and eyes that draw you in

**Outro**  
In the dream parlor on furs of darkest hue  
He inhales the powder, the world fractures anew
