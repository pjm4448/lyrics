# Leaning on Jehovah’s Care
### The Watchtower - January 2026

[Verse 1] 
When the shadows fall and hope feels far away,  
I kneel in prayer, knowing Jehovah hears me say,  
“My heart is heavy, but I trust in You,  
Your loyal love always sees me through.”

[Chorus]  
Leaning on Jehovah, I find strength to stand,  
He lifts my burdens with His gentle hand.  
His Word reminds me I am not alone,  
With faith and courage, I keep pressing on.

[Verse 2]  
Troubles may linger, but I will not lose heart,  
Jehovah’s spirit gives me a brand new start.  
I read His promises, they comfort me each day,  
With hope in His Kingdom, I walk in His way.

[Bridge] 
I cast my cares on Him, He knows my every need,  
Surrounded by my brothers, I find the love I need.

[Chorus]
Leaning on Jehovah, I find strength to stand,  
He lifts my burdens with His gentle hand.  
His Word reminds me I am not alone,  
With faith and courage, I am moving on.

[Production Notes] 
Tempo: Moderate (80–90 BPM), reflective and uplifting.  
Instrumentation: Acoustic guitar (primary), gentle piano, soft bass, light percussion (brushes or soft snare), and optional pedal steel for a classic country feel.  
Vocal Style: Key of G or A major. Male lead vocal; delivery should be warm, sincere, and clear, with heartfelt delivery and careful diction.  
Arrangement: Verses should be intimate, with instrumentation building in the chorus for encouragement and strength. The bridge may feature light harmonies or a soft instrumental solo.  
Overall Feel: Comforting and reassuring, suitable for personal reflection or congregation encouragement, always maintaining a respectful and uplifting tone.
