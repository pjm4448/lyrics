# "Voyager of the Deep"

**Verse 1:**
Where the horizon meets the endless blue
And morning light breaks silver through the waves
There rides a spirit born of salt and dream
Who charts the waters no one else would brave

**Chorus:**
Sail on, sail on, through canyons made of coral
Where ancient secrets whisper to the brave
The dolphins dance like laughter on the current
And every mile reveals what oceans gave

**Verse 2:**
Beneath the surface lies a world of wonder
Cathedral kelp that rises to the sun
The gentle giants sing their songs of distance
A thousand journeys woven into one

**Chorus:**
Sail on, sail on, through canyons made of coral

**Bridge:**
For those who hear the calling of the tides
Who trade their steady ground for rolling swells
The sea becomes a teacher and a mother
With mysteries that only she can tell

**Verse 3:**
So raise the anchor, set your course by starlight
Let compass needles guide you through the foam
The ocean asks for reverence and courage
And gives the wanderer a second home

**Outro:**
Sail on, sail on, you voyager of deep waters
The world beneath the blue is calling still
May those who follow honor what you've shown us
That wonder waits for those who have the will
