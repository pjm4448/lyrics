# "Speak the Truth Graciously"
### The Watchtower - January 2026

[Verse 1]
When I open up my heart to speak,
I pray for wisdom, gentle and meek.
Let my words be seasoned with kindness and truth,
Reflecting love in all I do.

[Chorus]
I want to speak the truth graciously,
With patience, peace, and dignity.
Let every word I say
Show love in every way,
So others see the hope inside of me.

[Verse 2]
Jesus showed the way with every word,
He taught with kindness, never stirred
By anger or by pride,
He let compassion guide,
And honored God in everything He heard.

[Chorus]
I want to speak the truth graciously,
With patience, peace, and dignity.
Let every word I say
Show love in every way,
So others see the hope inside of me.

[Bridge]
If I must correct, let me pray for care,
Remembering the mercy I’ve been shown.
May my words bring comfort, help to bear
The burdens others carry on their own.

[Chorus]
I want to speak the truth graciously,
With patience, peace, and dignity.
Let every word I say
Show love in every way,
So others see the hope inside of me.

[Outro]
Let my words reflect the truth from above,
Showing kindness and Jehovah’s love.

---

Production Notes / Musical Direction:
- Tempo: Moderate, gentle country ballad (around 80–90 BPM)
- Instrumentation: Acoustic guitar, light percussion, pedal steel, upright bass, subtle piano
- Vocal style: Key of G or A major. Male lead vocal; Warm, sincere, inviting; harmonies on chorus for emphasis
- Arrangement: Verses with simple guitar, chorus with fuller instrumentation, bridge with dynamic build, soft outro
- Mood: Uplifting, peaceful, and encouraging, matching the message of gracious, loving speech
