# "Carry Me Back to the Hollow"

**Verse 1:**
Morning mist on the ridgeline
Rolling hills turning gold
Winding paths through the timber
Stories my grandfather told
Wooden bridge by the farmhouse
Creek that sings over stone
Every mile that I wander
Makes me long for my own

**Chorus:**
Carry me back to the hollow
Down where the dogwoods bloom white
Back to the place where I started
Under those stars every night
Carry me home, carry me home

**Verse 2:**
I can taste the wild honey
Smell the pine on the air
Heard my mother in the kitchen
Felt her hands in my hair
Radio plays on the front porch
Fireflies dance in the yard
Life was simple and gentle
Before the world grew so hard

**Chorus:**
Carry me back to the hollow
Down where the dogwoods bloom white
Back to the place where I started
Under those stars every night
Carry me home, carry me home

**Bridge:**
I have seen distant oceans
Walked along city streets
Chased my share of distant fortunes
Tasted glory and defeat
But nothing compares to that valley
Where the earth and sky meet

**Verse 3:**
Now the years keep on turning
And I drift far and wide
But that land holds me steady
Like the moon pulls the tide
One day soon I will follow
That old familiar way
Back to the hills that first held me
And this time I will stay

**Final Chorus:**
Carry me back to the hollow
Down where the dogwoods bloom white
Back to the place I belong to
Under those stars every night
Carry me home, carry me home
Carry me home
