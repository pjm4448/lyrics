**Track 13 – Chapter 12: Tariq and the Berserker's Bloody Path**

**Verse 1**  
Tariq prowls the fetid underbelly of the city  
A gaunt grim wraith consumed by endless fury  
The Heart of the Berserker burns deep in his veins  
Each day the red powder feeds his growing pains  

**Pre-Chorus**  
Dreams of Borus and his jackal dogs still haunt his mind  
Now every stranger wears the face of those who left him behind  
He takes brutal work to fund his desperate hunt  
While crimson delusions blur the line between dream and front  

**Chorus**  
Down the berserker’s bloody path he blindly strides  
The powder turns his vengeance into savage wrath  
Innocents fall beneath his bloodstained hands  
As the line between dream and waking violently expands  

**Verse 2**  
In Kaelen’s reeking den he inhales the coarse red dust  
The world ignites once more with unrelenting lust  
A shadowed alley appears where a traitor stands alone  
Tariq charges roaring, crushing bone on stone  

**Bridge**  
The vagrant dies screaming beneath his crushing fists  
A young street youth is torn apart in the mist  
Kasim the fence falls in the Street of Silks so bright  
Fresh blood joins the old as sanity loses its light  

**Chorus**  
Down the berserker’s bloody path he blindly strides  
The powder turns his vengeance into savage wrath  
Innocents fall beneath his bloodstained hands  
As the line between dream and waking violently expands  

**Outro**  
He collapses in a squalid flophouse reeking of despair  
Blood crusted on his hands and madness thick in the air  
The faces of the dead now join his original sin  
While the carrion bird watches as his soul caves in  

**Production Notes**  
Berserker black metal with industrial metal elements at a driving 90-100 BPM that surges into blast beats during the bridge. Crushing down-tuned guitars and aggressive tribal war drums build raw intensity. Layer guttural roars, throat singing, and wet gore sound effects for the kills. Vocals shift from simmering growled verses to unhinged, bestial screams in the chorus and bridge. The outro drops to an exhausted, panting delivery with distant distorted bird cries mixed into low feedback. Overall production should feel chaotic, visceral, and increasingly unhinged, mirroring Tariq’s loss of control and descent into crimson madness.
