# Cunning Plan

[Verse 1 - Bells]
My master hired a servant Bob
Who turned out to be Kate
He thought he fancied servant boys
Which put him in a state
I said "just eat a lot of garlic
You won't want kissing anyone"
Then Flashheart crashed the wedding day
And stole the bride before the deed was done
I offered Edmund my own hand
The dress was there, why waste it?
Lord Percy wore one too, you see
We looked quite fetching, I must say

[Chorus]
'Cause I've got a cunning plan
(very cunning, sir)
It's clever in a turnip-flavoured way
I've got a cunning plan
So cunning it might just work today
So cunning you could put a tail on it
And call it a weasel
Or get us both beheaded

[Verse 2 - Head]
The Queen made Edmund chop off heads
He thought the job was grand
But then he axed Lord Ponsonby
Before the pardon was at hand
I said "just put a bag on him
And say he's grown a beard
A very short and leather-like one"
The Queen won't think that weird
Or draw a face upon a pumpkin
The problem's nearly cleared

[Verse 3 - Potato]
Sir Walter Raleigh brought a spud
The Queen thought him divine
My master burned with jealousy
And said "I'll sail the brine"
But Edmund hates the ocean
And Captain Rum had lost his way
I said "let's sail around to France
Buy foreign stuff and say
We found The Land Where People Speak French
And Have Good Cheese - hooray!"

[Chorus]
Yes, I've got a cunning plan
(geographical, sir)
Why discover lands when France is near?
I've got a cunning plan
They've got good cheese and atmosphere
The Queen can't read a map
We'll buy things from a London shop
And put them on a foreign map

[Verse 4 - Money]
My master owed a thousand pounds
To a Bishop straight from hell
Who'd burn his bits with pokers
If he didn't pay up well
Lord Percy made some "purest green"
A worthless sticky lump
I said "we'll sell it as a jewel
A Greeny-Thing - the rich are chumps!"

[Bridge - Spoken]
And here's my backup plan for cash:
Step one: I become a professional dog
Step two: People pay to look at me
That's passive income, that is. Very modern.

[Chorus - Beer]
Still, I've got a cunning plan
(theological, sir)
When puritans and boozers share a hall
I've got a cunning plan
My master ran between them through it all
Eating turnips, downing ale
The Whiteadders heard the noise - I said
"Heavenly angels arguing how much they love being sober!"
It almost worked until they found the door
And saw Lord Melchett on the floor

[Verse 5 - Chains]
The Spanish locked us in a cell
Prince Ludwig had us caught
That master of disguise had plans
To kill us both, we thought
While Melchett lost his noble mind
I had ideas galore
I said "let's wait until we're dead
They won't want corpses anymore"
Or else we stand extremely still
Pretend we're two small ugly walls
They'll walk straight past - but Ludwig won
And poison took them all

[Final Chorus]
'Cause I've got a cunning plan
(architectural, sir)
I'm basically a wall with even less hygiene
I've got a cunning plan
The finest that this century has seen
The whole cast died that day
A dark and comic ending
To the Elizabethan way
