**Track 12 – Chapter 11: Amina and the Necropolis of Madness**

**Verse 1**  
The silence of the necropolis presses down like stone  
Amina kneels before the markers of those she has known  
Sorab, Yasmin and little Kavi cold beneath the clay  
She traces their names as her world has slipped away  

**Pre-Chorus**  
The wooden horse clutched tight against her hollow chest  
Worn smooth by endless tears and grief that will not rest  
Farid’s grey powder offered comfort that proved false  
Only deepening the void with its cold and silent pulse  

**Chorus**  
In the necropolis of madness sorrow turns to stone  
Where Zaltus brings his fevered rite beneath the starless dome  
He seeks the Sleeper in a widow’s anguished cries  
Chaining her grief to awaken what forever lies  

**Verse 2**  
Under moonless sky the captain and his men appear  
They seize her roughly, dragging her from all she holds dear  
Chained before the graves where her family lies in peace  
Zaltus chants of the Three while her terror will not cease  

**Bridge**  
He presses the sigil shard against her trembling brow  
Crushes cloying herbs as he screams his fevered vow  
The Sleeper must awaken when the Three are made as one  
But only blood and silence answer beneath the weeping sun  

**Chorus**  
In the necropolis of madness sorrow turns to stone  
Where Zaltus brings his fevered rite beneath the starless dome  
He seeks the Sleeper in a widow’s anguished cries  
Chaining her grief to awaken what forever lies  

**Outro**  
Violence erupts as his own men turn against the rite  
Zaltus strikes them down in fury beneath the starless night  
Amina huddles broken by the graves she holds so dear  
While the carrion bird circles slowly, drawing ever near  

**Production Notes**  
Grey cinematic ambient with dark funeral drone elements at an extremely slow 40-55 BPM. Minimal percussion, relying on distant tolling bells, cold wind textures, and a single mourning cello that carries long, sustained notes. Zaltus’s arrival and ritual use low, menacing brass stabs and a throbbing sub-bass pulse for the sigil and chant. The violence in the bridge features sudden percussive impacts and raw, chaotic shouts that quickly subside back into oppressive silence. Vocals are fragile and whispered for Amina’s parts, shifting to a harsh, fevered growl for Zaltus’s lines. The outro returns to sparse, hollow textures with faint circling wing sounds. Overall production should feel damp, cold, and suffocating, emphasizing isolation, violated grief, and encroaching madness.
