# "Where the Timberline Meets Heaven"

**Verse 1:**
He was raised among the flatlands
Where the sky stretched endlessly
But he always felt a longing
For a place he'd never seen
At twenty-five he drove westward
Chasing something he couldn't name
When he saw those peaks at sunrise
He knew nothing stays the same

**Verse 2:**
He learned to read the aspen
How they shimmer gold and green
Learned to walk through cathedral forests
Find the clearings in between
Watched the hawks ride thermal currents
Saw the elk in morning mist
Felt a silence so complete there
Like a prayer upon his lips

**Chorus:**
Where the timberline meets heaven
And the air is thin and clear
Where the stars feel close enough to touch
And the world just disappears
He found something in those highlands
That the lowlands couldn't give
Where the timberline meets heaven
He finally learned to live

**Verse 3:**
Now he's seen a hundred snowfalls

But he's also seen the fences
Seen the condos start to climb
Seen the wilderness grow smaller
Just a little at a time
