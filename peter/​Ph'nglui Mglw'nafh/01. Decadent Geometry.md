[Verse 1]
[Clean, melancholic guitars. Whispered vocals. 7/8 time]
A lockbox in the study, dust upon the keys
Stopped hearts lying cold beneath the Providence trees
I sift the dark ephemera of a mind that dug too deep
And find a horrid symmetry in what the shadows keep.
Yellowed clippings form a sigil, a puzzle incomplete
A trail of sudden madness spilling to the street.

​[Pre-Chorus]
[Tempo building. Mellotron swells.]
A decadent geometry, defying all design
A mystic current dragging down the fragile human line.

​[Chorus]
[Shift to Heavy 4/4 time. Loud, theatrical wail.]
The horror in the clay, carved by fevered hands
A psychic broadcast rippling across the sleeping lands
Monoliths of damp green stone rising in the brain
We are but receivers in the architecture of the insane.
We build his shadowed temples while we dream.

​[Verse 2]
[Return to 7/8 time. Clean guitars. Whispered, strained vocals.]
The sculptor spoke of cities beneath a sickly moon
Of angles badly broken, and a maddening, low croon.
In his fevered dark delirium, the abyssal truth was cast,
A dread so vastly older than the oceans of our past.
From rotting Bayou altars to the madhouse cell
The sensitives awaken to the tolling of his bell.

​[Bridge]
[Heavy Distorted Bass, Crescendo, Dissonant Synths]
The artists in their garrets, the painters going blind
Catching fragments of a signal meant to terrify the mind.
Whispers of the ancient ones, leaking through the veil,
As the boundaries of reason stagger, fracture, and fail.

​[Chorus]
[Heavy 4/4 time. Loud, theatrical wail. Oceanic textures.]
The horror in the clay, carved by fevered hands
A psychic broadcast rippling across the sleeping lands
Monoliths of damp green stone rising in the brain
We are but receivers in the architecture of the insane.
We build his shadowed temples while we dream.

​[Outro]
[Instruments cut out. Single ambient drone fading to ocean waves.]
The box is closed.
The manuscript remains.
The ambient hum of the waking rains.
He waits. He dreams.
He waits. He dreams.
