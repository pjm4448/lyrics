[Verse 1]
Candles bleed across the table
in halls that never end
I fill your cup with liquid midnight
and watch the daylight bend
Your pulse forgets the world outside
these walls of mirrored glass
Step nearer, let my fingers trace
the future you will never have

[Pre-Chorus]
Taste the sweetness on the edge
It hides a deeper blade

[Chorus]
Come closer, let the darkness fold
Wear the thorns that shine like gold
Every breath a slow surrender
I raise you up
then pull you under
We move through gardens made of frost
Where beauty pays its final cost

[Verse 2]
Marble statues watch in silence
as your shadow starts to fray
I kiss the lies from off your lips
and steal the words you cannot say
You dance beneath the crystal chandeliers
that drip with melted years
The laughter here is counterfeit
yet still you chase the tears

[Bridge]
You asked for more than life could give
and life was quick to leave
Now every jewel around your neck
becomes another chain you weave

[Chorus]
Come closer, let the darkness fold
Wear the thorns that shine like gold
We move through gardens made of frost
Where even gods have long since lost

[Outro]
The masks are cracking one by one
your reflection turns away
The garden freezes where you stand
and night forgets your name
Nothing left but fading perfume
and the echo of a flame
Nothing left
but the cold
remains
