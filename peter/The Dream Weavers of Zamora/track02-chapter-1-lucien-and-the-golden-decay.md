**Track 2 – Chapter 1: Lucien and the Golden Decay**

**Verse 1**  
In the House of Ormuz, a cage of darkened wood  
Lucien the merchant heir sits where his father stood  
Sea green silks hang loose upon his thinning frame  
Wealth without hunger, a soul consumed by shame  

**Pre-Chorus**  
Slave girls lounge like soulless painted things  
His gaze drifts vacant over forgotten kings  
He shatters a jade idol with a languid hand  
Yet the broken shards bring no thrill to this empty man  

**Chorus**  
Golden decay, golden decay  
Jaded heart that nothing can sway  
He craves the dust that promises release  
Aurea’s golden shimmer, a sweet and fleeting peace  

**Verse 2**  
Jafar the Gilded Tongue slithers through the door  
Robes of old gold with patterns that twist once more  
Metallic sweetness clings to his ageless skin  
Long golden nails and eyes that draw you in  

**Bridge**  
From a carved wooden box the Dust of Aurea gleams  
Black Lotus refined through forbidden dreams  
First taste is free for the noble lost in ennui  
Breathe deep, my lord, and let the visions set you free  

**Chorus**  
Golden decay, golden decay  
Jaded heart that nothing can sway  
He craves the dust that promises release  
Aurea’s golden shimmer, a sweet and fleeting peace  

**Outro**  
In the dream parlor on furs of darkest hue  
He inhales the powder, the world fractures anew  
A feasting hall of sunlight where companions gleam too bright  
Golden smears on tables that bleed into the night  
He wakes with trembling fingers marked in shining gold  
The line between dream and waking now grows cold  

**Production Notes**  
Decadent darkwave with gothic synth elements at a slow 65-75 BPM. Pulsing arpeggiated synths in a minor key create a shimmering golden effect using layered saw waves drenched in lush reverb and subtle delay. Add breathy, seductive female backing vocals that gradually turn mocking and hollow. Use cold, elegant piano strikes for the jade figurine shattering moment. Keep the verses intimate and spoken-sung with light distortion on Lucien's lines to show growing detachment. The chorus swells with richer pads and a faint metallic chime for Jafar's presence. In the outro, introduce a disorienting, swirling synth transition to represent the dream collapse, ending with a faint golden shimmer effect (high-frequency sparkle) layered under a single sustained, uneasy chord. Overall production should feel opulent yet decaying, drawing the listener from boredom into dangerous temptation. Vocals remain low and intimate throughout to mirror Lucien's listless drawl.
