# "Threefold Cord"
### The Watchtower - April 2026

### VERSE 1
We didn't walk this road by chance,
We chose to build on higher ground.
A friend of God beside me stands,
In You our common faith was found.

Not just a feeling, not just a vow,
But something deeper, something real.
You teach us how to love each day,
A bond that time cannot steal.

---

### CHORUS
We are a threefold cord,
Not quickly torn apart.
**Jehovah woven through the centre,**
You bind us heart to heart.
One flesh, one hope, one life we share,
Through every storm we'll stand.
We are a threefold cord,
Bound by Your faithful hand.

---

### VERSE 2
We carve out time, we lay things down,
We learn to love what matters most.
Through prayer we know each other's heart,
In You we never walk alone.

No distance, no distraction wins,
We're building something meant to last.
**Where patience grows and kindness wins,**
Your love's the anchor, holding fast.

---

### BRIDGE
When the cracks begin to show,
We won't look for a way out.
We'll find a way back home—
We'll walk restoration's road.

Like a masterpiece worth saving,
Worth the work, worth the wait,
So we'll keep on trusting, keep on praying,
Through You we'll find the way.

---

### FINAL CHORUS
We are a threefold cord,
Not quickly torn apart.
Jehovah woven through the centre,
You bind us heart to heart.
One flesh, one hope, one life we share,
Through every storm we'll stand.
We are a threefold cord,
Bound by Your faithful hand.
