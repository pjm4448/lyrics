**Track 11 – Chapter 10: Zaltus and the Beggar's Misdirection**

**Verse 1**  
Zamora offers Zaltus only deepening despair  
His Black Hand empire fractures beyond repair  
Whispers of a Sleeper and a power long denied  
Drive the broken captain through the city's rotten hide  

**Pre-Chorus**  
In a refuse-choked alley by the foetid docks  
He finds the Oracle of Bones among verminous rags and rocks  
Milky eyes stare vacant yet pierce into his soul  
A twisted pawn of the Weavers playing a deadly role  

**Chorus**  
Riddles spill from the beggar, cold and sly  
The woman's tears, the graves' silence, the sigil's light  
Bind them with blood and the Sleeper wakes tonight  
A false path woven in the Weavers' dark design  

**Verse 2**  
The beggar presses a shard of dark rough pottery  
Etched with the star-like sigil that pulses unnaturally  
Seek the weeping widow where the dead whisper low  
Her endless sorrow is the key to the power you must know  

**Bridge**  
Zaltus clutches the shard as its heat sears his skin  
Madness surges stronger as the clues sink in  
Amina in the necropolis, her grief a sacred flame  
He will bind her sorrow and seize the Sleeper's name  

**Chorus**  
Riddles spill from the beggar, cold and sly  
The woman's tears, the graves' silence, the sigil's light  
Bind them with blood and the Sleeper wakes tonight  
A false path woven in the Weavers' dark design  

**Outro**  
He staggers back into the labyrinthine stinking maze  
Mind a black whirlwind of desperate hope and craze  
Unseen in the shadows the Silent One nods with cold delight  
While above the carrion bird watches with ancient appetite  

**Production Notes**  
Dark ambient folk with paranoid ritual elements at a slow 60-70 BPM. Sparse bone percussion and dry, rasping vocal samples evoke the beggar's twisted speech. Low sub-bass throbs pulse in time with the sigil's unnatural heat. Whispered layered voices create a sense of insidious manipulation. Vocals are delivered in a low, growling tone that grows increasingly unhinged and fevered. The outro introduces faint circling wing sounds and a cold, distant bird cry mixed with low drones. Overall production should feel claustrophobic and deceptive, building a sense of creeping madness and inevitable entrapment as Zaltus is led deeper into the Weavers' snare.
